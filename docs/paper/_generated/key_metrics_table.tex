E@90\% of best & First epoch where a run's validation metric reaches \(0.9\times\) its best value. & Computed per seed; we report mean $\pm$ 95\% t-CI over matched seeds. \\
Early slope (e1→e3) & Finite difference between epochs 1 and 3 of the validation metric. & Captures initial learning speed; positive is faster. \\
Stability CV & Coefficient of variation of validation loss across epochs in a run. & Lower is steadier; we average CV across matched seeds. \\
Alignment AUC & Normalized area under the layerwise cosine-alignment curves \(\rho_\ell(t)\). & Reported as mean $\pm$ 95\% t-CI; higher implies backprop-like signals. \\
TOST equivalence & Two one-sided tests on matched seeds with \(\delta=\pm0.5\) pp for accuracy (\(\pm0.01\) for $R^2$). & We declare equivalence when both one-sided p-values < 0.05. \\
